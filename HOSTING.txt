Build: node build.cjs
Publish only dist/, never the entire workspace. Existing unused artwork stays in
the source folder and is excluded from the deployment package.

Netlify: use the included netlify.toml or upload dist/. Enable form detection in
the Netlify dashboard, redeploy, and configure submission notifications. Send one
real test after deployment and confirm it appears in Forms before launch.
https://docs.netlify.com/manage/forms/setup/

The contact form needs Netlify Forms. Other static hosts can serve the site, but
require a separate form backend. Until connected, the JavaScript displays the
direct email address and does not simulate successful submission.

Connect the purchased domain through the host dashboard and enable HTTPS and
HTTP-to-HTTPS redirects. Verify the _headers rules in live HTTP responses;
other hosts may require equivalent configuration. Inline styles remain allowed
for existing layout markup; inline scripts and external scripts are blocked.

After choosing the final domain, add its canonical URL and sitemap. Confirm the
business details and marketing claims before publishing. Google Fonts remains
an external font dependency, with local fallback fonts.

Verify desktop/mobile navigation, all service dialogs and keyboard navigation,
reduced-motion settings, failed form requests, and successful form delivery.
Client-side field limits are usability controls, not server-side validation.
